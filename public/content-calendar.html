👋 Quick user guide

Your 90-day content plan at a glance.

  1. It opens on today — tap any card to preview it on the right.
  2. Use List/Grid, search and platform filter to find any post.
  3. Month carousel, ‹ Prev / Next › and Today move you around.
  4. Tap 🔒 Unlock (admin password) before editing anything.
  5. Per day: edit text, tap platform icons, set a time, add resources and todos — all autosaved.
  6. Drag cards onto each other (or Move ◀ ▶) to reschedule; finishing all todos auto-marks Posted.
  7. Scheduled tab is the timed queue; Reports has charts and CSV export.
  8. Use Backup regularly to safeguard your data.
